What is Conflict Coaching?

Conflict Coaching is a private, one-on-one service that helps individuals better understand conflict, strengthen communication, prepare for difficult conversations, and determine appropriate next steps.

Unlike mediation, Conflict Coaching involves only one participant while maintaining the same commitment to neutrality, participant self-determination, respectful communication, and thoughtful dialogue.

The purpose is not to tell you what decisions to make. The purpose is to help you think more clearly about the conflict you are experiencing, recognize additional possibilities, and prepare yourself for productive conversations.

Who May Benefit?

  • Workplace conflict
  • Family disagreements
  • Co-parenting communication
  • Neighbor disputes
  • Organizational conflict
  • Leadership communication
  • Difficult conversations
  • Preparing for mediation
  • Communication breakdowns
  • High-conflict relationships

What We'll Explore Together

Every conflict is unique. Rather than following a rigid formula, Conflict Coaching provides a structured conversation tailored to your circumstances, concerns, and goals.

Depending on your situation, we may explore topics such as:

  • Clarifying your goals and priorities
  • Understanding positions and underlying interests
  • Recognizing communication barriers
  • Preparing for difficult conversations
  • Perspective-taking and understanding other viewpoints
  • Managing emotions during conflict
  • Developing communication strategies
  • Determining whether mediation may be appropriate

What Conflict Coaching Is Not

Conflict Coaching is a professional conflict-management service. While meaningful conversations often involve emotions, the service has important boundaries.

Conflict Coaching is not:

  • Therapy
  • Counseling
  • Legal representation
  • Legal advice
  • Crisis intervention
  • Mental health treatment
  • Advocacy for one side of a dispute

The focus remains helping participants think more clearly about conflict while preserving participant self-determination and supporting thoughtful decision-making.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Conflict Coaching?

Conflict Coaching is a private, one-on-one service that helps individuals better understand conflict, strengthen communication, prepare for difficult conversations, and determine whether mediation or another process may be appropriate.

Do both people have to participate?

No. Unlike mediation, Conflict Coaching involves only one participant. The service focuses on helping you better understand the conflict and prepare for future communication.

Is Conflict Coaching therapy?

No. Conflict Coaching is not therapy or mental health treatment. It focuses on conflict management, communication, and thoughtful decision-making.

Can Conflict Coaching lead to mediation?

Yes. In some situations, Conflict Coaching helps participants determine that mediation may be an appropriate next step. In other situations, coaching itself may provide sufficient preparation for future conversations.
